# Anonymous Feedback Done Right

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Style:** Use case story with product shots
**Music:** Thoughtful, builds to positive resolution

---

## Script

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Person looking hesitant at laptop | "Your team has opinions. But are they sharing them?" |
| 0:05-0:10 | Meeting room scene: people nodding, not speaking | "In meetings, people nod along. In surveys, they give safe answers." |
| 0:10-0:15 | Text on screen: "What would they say if no one was watching?" | "What would they say if they knew — really knew — it was anonymous?" |
| 0:15-0:20 | Mosaic privacy settings, highlight "Fully Anonymous" | "Mosaic offers true anonymity. Not just a promise — a guarantee." |
| 0:20-0:25 | Participant view showing privacy badge | "Participants see exactly how their privacy is protected. Before they respond." |
| 0:25-0:30 | Animation: response going into a vault/black box | "Fully anonymous means even you can't see who said what." |
| 0:30-0:35 | Contrast: other mode "Optional Reveal" | "Or let people choose to reveal themselves — their call, not yours." |
| 0:35-0:42 | Real responses appearing (blurred/example text) | "When people trust the process, they share what matters." |
| 0:42-0:48 | Analysis showing honest, actionable themes | "And honest feedback leads to real insights." |
| 0:48-0:55 | Person looking at results, nodding with understanding | "Now you know what your team actually thinks." |
| 0:55-1:00 | Logo + "Feedback people trust to give" | "Mosaic. Feedback people trust to give." |

---

## Production Notes

- Opening should feel relatable (we've all been in those meetings)
- Privacy section should feel secure, trustworthy (blues, locks, shields)
- Transition from "safe answers" to "real answers" should be visible
- End on positive, empowered note

## Scenes to Capture

1. Meeting room B-roll (stock footage OK)
2. Mosaic privacy settings (all three levels)
3. Participant trust screen
4. Response flow animation
5. Analysis results

## Key Messages

1. **Trust is earned** - We show participants how they're protected
2. **Options matter** - Different situations need different privacy levels
3. **Honesty = value** - Anonymous feedback is more actionable
